Goal: 1,000 builders

The goal is to find and train 1,000 people who go on to build and lead companies. Most of them are between 12 and 18 today. What they become depends on who finds them, who teaches them and whether they get real work to do.

West Ventures is set up to do those three things.

Precedent: the workshop

Leonardo trained in Verrocchio’s shop in Florence and painted parts of real commissions as a teenager. Ghirlandaio, from the same circle, took on Michelangelo at thirteen. A few rooms like that produced much of the High Renaissance.

Two long-running studies: ability can be identified early, and outcomes depend heavily on what a child is exposed to.

Model

  • A founder brings a real problem. We don’t charge them.
  • A team of six works on it for a full semester, for course credit.
  • Partner campuses send their best students to join the teams.

Risks

Project supplyBy 2033 we’d need about 200 projects a semester, roughly ten times what we run today.
ScoutingOur scouting is young. Builder’s Quotient still has to prove it can spot talent earlier than referrals do.
